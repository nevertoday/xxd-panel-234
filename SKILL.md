## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a modern graphic key visual with a digital-editorial interface feeling: large flat colour planes, simplified contours, fine lines and geometric symbols. Use an asymmetric modular grid. The subject is the only visual core. A few rounded cards, partial frames, fine rules or micro icons may structure the page, but they must not become a complex UI. Flat, matte, almost no traditional shading. Colours are the brightest living hues from the source, clarified, with lots of warm white: about one or two main colours, one support and a tiny accent. Typography is small, clear, narrow sans or light mono. It is not a realistic illustration, a cyber interface, a dense app UI, a game screen, a cartoon sticker or a gradient glow.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-234/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
